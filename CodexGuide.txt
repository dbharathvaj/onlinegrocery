# MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT
# D.K. RAJU GENERAL STORE – COMPLETE FULL-STACK ONLINE GROCERY PLATFORM

Act as a senior full-stack software engineer, software architect, UI/UX designer and database engineer.

I want you to design and develop a complete, production-oriented full-stack online grocery ordering platform for my existing physical kirana shop.

This is a real business application intended for real customers. Do not create a static demo, frontend-only prototype or college project.

The website must be extremely easy to use for everyone, including elderly people, rural customers, Telugu-speaking users and people with limited technical knowledge.

The business name is:

**D.K. Raju General Store**

Business location: Village-based kirana shop in Andhra Pradesh, India.

Business model:
- The shop owner uploads products and prices.
- Customers browse available products online.
- Customers order groceries from their homes.
- The shop owner receives and manages orders.
- The shop prepares and delivers groceries to customers.
- Customers can pay through Cash on Delivery or online UPI payment.

The platform must support customers within the shop's configured delivery service area.
